Where donations go

Practical support with community impact

Donations can help fund the basic but essential needs that sustain the league and expand opportunity.

  • Referees and match operations
  • Goal nets, bibs, and footballs
  • School support for girls most in need
  • Public holiday tournaments and meals
  • Coach training and development
  • Street cleanups and community activities
  • Recognition events and medals

Why give

Your support helps turn football into a platform for safety, leadership, and growth.

Why now

Timely support helps maintain momentum, sustain girls’ participation, and strengthen local impact.

Why this matters

For many youth, access to structure and support can shape the direction of their future.
