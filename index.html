Our mission

Using football to create real social change

Inter Base League was founded to provide a meaningful alternative to crime, substance abuse, and hopelessness in Kibera. Through football, youth gain structure, teamwork, positive role models, and a stronger sense of belonging.

The league also supports girls through a dedicated women’s division focused on confidence, opportunity, leadership, and long-term development.
