Programs

Support that goes beyond the pitch.

Inter Base League combines football with mentorship, education, community work, and girls’ empowerment to create more lasting impact.

Program areas

What donations can help fund

Weekend league matches

Regular structured football on weekends helps keep youth engaged in a positive environment.

Women’s league support

Girls gain access to sport, visibility, teamwork, and confidence through organized competition.

Public holiday tournaments

Event days bring football, meals, and mentorship together during school breaks.

Mentorship sessions

Role models and community leaders help girls and youth think about health, equality, and leadership.

Street cleanups

Monthly cleanups build pride, service, responsibility, and stronger community ties.

Coach development

Training for coaches helps improve the quality and sustainability of football programming.

Equipment and supplies

Footballs, nets, bibs, and other essentials are necessary to run safe and organized matches.

School support

Helping girls remain in school protects long-term opportunity and reduces vulnerability.

Recognition events

End-of-season ceremonies and medals help honor effort, achievement, and community pride.

Why it matters

Holistic support creates stronger outcomes

Football is the entry point, but real transformation requires more than matches. When youth also receive mentorship, educational support, better coaching, and community engagement, the impact becomes deeper and more sustainable.

That is what makes Inter Base League more than a sports program.
