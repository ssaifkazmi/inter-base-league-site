About us

Built inside the community it serves.

Inter Base League is an independently run football league in Kibera, Nairobi, founded to help young people access structure, support, purpose, and opportunity.

Background

A local initiative with a growing impact

Founded in 2019, Inter Base League was created to combat substance abuse, criminality, and teenage pregnancies through football. It has grown into a respected community initiative engaging youth across multiple divisions.

The league has expanded to include a women’s division, which plays an essential role in giving girls greater access to sport, teamwork, mentorship, and confidence.

Beyond football, the league supports community development through cleanups, educational support, and partnerships that strengthen long-term opportunity.

Vision

Developing talent and leadership

To become a leading talent development and youth empowerment vehicle for Kibera through football, community engagement, and mentorship.

Mission

A positive path forward

To help emancipate youth from crime and substance abuse through football, teamwork, discipline, and stronger community support systems.

Core values

What guides the league

Hard work

Commitment and professionalism are central to how the league develops players and teams.

Wellness

Physical and mental wellbeing matter in building healthier individuals and communities.

Community

Football becomes more powerful when it is connected to service, education, and belonging.

Community impact

Football as a platform for belonging and growth

Inter Base League is about more than the game itself. It creates space for young people to spend time productively, connect with teammates and mentors, and build confidence in a structured environment.

The women’s league is especially important in expanding access and visibility for girls.
